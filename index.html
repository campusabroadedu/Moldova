Government Medical University in Moldova.

Established in 1945, Nicolae Testemitanu State University of Medicine and Pharmacy (USMF) is a premier national medical university in Eastern Europe. Experience a 6-year English-medium MD program designed for global medical practice.[cite: 71]

Program & Tuition Details

The MD syllabus is highly compatible with USMLE and PLAB, making it a gateway to practice in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. Graduates are eligible to practice anywhere in Europe.[cite: 71]

6-Year MD Program

Undergraduate course in Medicine (MD Study) taught 100% in the English medium.[cite: 71]

Transparent Tuition

4,000 € for the first year, and 4,300 € per year from the 2nd to the 6th year of study.[cite: 71]

Global Approvals & Recognitions

The university holds international institutional accreditations and is recognized by top global medical councils, allowing graduates to practice worldwide.

  • WHO / WDOMS Listed Listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools published by the World Health Organization (WHO).[cite: 71]
  • ECFMG (USA) Recognition Students and graduates are eligible to apply to the ECFMG for certification and examination in the United States.[cite: 71]
  • WFME Accreditation via IAAR Holds an international institutional accreditation certificate according to the standards of the World Federation for Medical Education (WFME).[cite: 71]
  • NMC India & European Councils Recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) of India and affiliated with the Association of Medical Schools in Europe (AMSE).[cite: 71]

Hostel, Campus & Student Life

Located in Chisinau with a moderate continental climate, USMF accommodates over 6,000 students from 33 different countries in a safe and highly equipped campus environment.[cite: 71]

Accommodation

Separate hostels for boys and girls with single, double, and triple rooms. Equipped with kitchens, Wi-Fi, and study rooms within walking distance of the university clinics.[cite: 71]

Indian Mess

Authentic Indian and Kerala food is available for students. Celebrate festivals like Onam, Holi, and Bakrid together with the Moldova Indian Students Association (MISHA).[cite: 71]

Clinical Exposure

The Faculty of Medicine includes 50 departments and 35 hospitals with a 10,000-bed capacity. Practical training is prioritized.[cite: 71]

Medical Library

The Scientific Medical Library holds over 1,000,000 textbooks, digital catalogs, and biomedical databases.[cite: 71]
